Deleted the thin coding-agent InteractiveMode, its package subpath, and its tests. `AgentSession` is again the shared business boundary.

`packages/tui/src/interactive/interactive-mode.ts` now owns the terminal application. Its instance-scoped `InteractiveStore` binds/replaces the current AgentSession, rejects stale events, owns bounded terminal presentation state, and delegates prompt/queue/abort operations directly to AgentSession. Nano Stores remain exclusive to packages/tui.

The CLI dynamically imports `runTui({ session })` and disposes the AgentSession it creates. Future print/RPC modes can operate directly over AgentSession without loading OpenTUI or sharing terminal state.

Rewrote ADR 0007 around the Pi architecture lesson, amended ADRs 0003/0005/0006, and updated architecture, terminal architecture, terminology, workspace rules, README, and roadmap. Session replacement binds the new subscription before notifying render observers, so event ownership changes atomically.
